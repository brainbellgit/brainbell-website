How to Set Up Your Mindfulness Routine in BrainBell

Simple step-by-step guide

  1. Pick a Mindfulness Activity
    Choose a gentle focus like Breathing, Take a Walk, Drink Water, or Short Break.
  2. Set Your Reminder Time
    Decide when you'd like BrainBell to remind you — morning stretch, midday calm, or evening pause.
  3. Start the Moment
    Tap "Start" to begin. For breathing sessions, a calming animation will guide you in and out with ease. You can pause or repeat anytime.
  4. Use the Mindfulness Clock
    BrainBell includes a visual timer to help you manage short breaks throughout your day — perfect for stepping away without losing focus.
  5. Need a Gentle Push?
    Chat with your built-in AI Mindfulness Coach anytime for support, motivation, or guidance when things feel overwhelming.
