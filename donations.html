Company's Charitable Giving

A Commitment to Humanitarian Aid: 10% for MSF

BrainBell direct 10% of our monthly income to support Médecins Sans Frontières (MSF / Doctors Without Borders).

This organization delivers urgent medical humanitarian aid to people affected by crises worldwide—from wars and epidemics to natural disasters.

Our partnership reflects our belief in MSF's mission to provide life-saving care, wherever it's needed most.
