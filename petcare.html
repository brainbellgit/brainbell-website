How to Set Up PetCare in BrainBell

Simple step-by-step guide

  1. Add Your Pet
    Tap the "+" button to begin adding your pet.
  2. Upload a Photo
    Choose a picture of your pet to personalize their profile.
  3. Enter Pet Details
    Fill in your pet's name, type, breed, and birth date. Then tap Save.
  4. Access Your Pet Profile
    Your pet's profile will appear. Tap to open it.
  5. Set Up Routines
    Start creating reminders for your pet's care — like feeding, walks, grooming, vaccinations, and more.
  6. Customize Schedules
    Choose how often each reminder repeats — daily, weekly, or just once.
  7. Track Milestones
    Record special moments and progress by adding a milestone. Select a category such as Training, Health, Social, or Achievements.
  8. Write Pet Journals
    Keep a personal blog of your pet's journey — share stories, track habits, or note progress.
