🌙 The Quiet Struggle of Remembering Everything

A message to every overwhelmed parent: You're not failing. You're just doing too much, with too little support.

You’re juggling schedules, wiping tiny faces, cleaning up spilled cereal, replying to work emails, and somehow still trying to remember if your child napped long enough today.

You tell yourself, “I should be able to remember this.”
But the truth is — no one is built to carry it all in their head.

That’s why we created BrainBell.
Not to replace you. Not to judge you.
But to gently whisper the things you meant to remember — the nap times, the feeding routine, the milestone check-ins.

Because your child’s growth deserves to be noticed — and so do you.
Because your mental space is sacred — and so is your well-being.
Because remembering everything shouldn’t be a burden.

BrainBell is here for the late nights, the forgetful mornings, the scattered days.
It’s your quiet helper in the background — reminding you of what matters, so you can focus on being present, not perfect.

✨ You’re doing your best. Let us help with the rest.

How to Set Up Parenting in BrainBell

Simple step-by-step guide

Add Your Child

Tap the "+" button to create a profile for your child.

Enter Details

Fill in your child's name, date of birth, and optionally, upload a photo. Then tap Save.

Add a Feeding Entry

Scroll down and tap "Add Feed" to begin logging your child's feeding routine.

Choose a Category

Select the type of feed—for example: solid food, milk, or snack.

Add Details

Input the amount, any notes, or important information you'd like to track.

Set a Feeding Schedule

Choose the time(s) you usually feed your child—such as breakfast, lunch, and dinner. You can add multiple times if needed.

Enable Daily Reminders

Tap the Repeat button to receive automatic daily reminders, so you never miss a feeding time.
