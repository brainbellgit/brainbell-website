Privacy Policy

Your privacy is our priority. We believe in transparency, simplicity, and respect for your personal information.

🔒Our Commitment to Your Privacy

At Brainbell, we are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy outlines our practices regarding data collection, use, and disclosure. We believe that privacy is a fundamental right, and we have designed our service with this principle at its core.

This policy applies to all users of Brainbell, including those who access our website, mobile application, and related services. By using Brainbell, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

📊Data Collection

We do not collect, store, or process any personal data from our users.

Brainbell operates with a fundamental commitment to privacy: we do not collect, store, transmit, or process any personal information, user data, or usage analytics. Our application is designed to function entirely on your device, ensuring that your reminders, schedules, and personal information remain private and secure.

What This Means

  • No Account Creation: Brainbell does not require you to create an account or provide any personal information to use our services.
  • No Data Transmission: All data entered into Brainbell remains on your device and is never transmitted to our servers or third parties.
  • No Tracking: We do not use cookies, tracking pixels, analytics tools, or any other technologies to monitor your usage or behavior.
  • No Third-Party Sharing: Since we do not collect data, there is no data to share with third parties, advertisers, or partners.

💾Local Device Storage

Brainbell stores all reminder data, schedules, and preferences exclusively on your device using local storage mechanisms. This includes:

  • Reminder schedules and notifications
  • Personal preferences and settings
  • Any notes or information you choose to enter

This data is stored locally on your device and is never synchronized with external servers or cloud services. You have complete control over this data, and you can delete it at any time by uninstalling the application or clearing the app's data through your device settings.

🌐Website Usage

When you visit our website, we may collect minimal technical information that is necessary for the website to function properly. This includes:

  • Basic connection information (IP address, browser type) that is automatically transmitted by your browser
  • Pages visited and time spent on pages (through standard web server logs)

However, we do not use this information to identify individual users, and we do not combine it with any other data sources. Our website does not use cookies for tracking purposes, and we do not employ third-party analytics services.

📧Contact Information

If you choose to contact us through our website's contact form or email, we will receive the information you voluntarily provide, such as your name and email address. This information is used solely to respond to your inquiry and is not used for marketing purposes or shared with third parties.

You are under no obligation to provide contact information, and you may use Brainbell without ever contacting us.

👶Children's Privacy

Brainbell is designed to be safe for users of all ages. Since we do not collect any personal information, we do not knowingly collect information from children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction).

Parents and guardians can be assured that their children's use of Brainbell does not involve any data collection or privacy risks.

📝Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.

We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically to stay informed about how we protect your privacy. Your continued use of Brainbell after any changes to this policy constitutes your acceptance of those changes.

⚖️Your Rights

Since we do not collect or store your personal data, there is no data for us to provide, modify, or delete. However, you maintain complete control over:

  • All data stored locally on your device
  • Your decision to use or discontinue using Brainbell
  • Any information you choose to share with us through contact forms

You can delete all Brainbell data at any time by uninstalling the application from your device.

Questions About Privacy?

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or our privacy practices, please do not hesitate to contact us.
